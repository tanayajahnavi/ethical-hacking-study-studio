NMAP · HOST DISCOVERY01

Discovery vs. scanning

-sn performs host discovery without port scanning. -Pn skips host discovery and treats targets as online for the requested scan.

-PEICMP Echo probe
-PSTCP SYN discovery probe
-PATCP ACK discovery probe
-PUUDP discovery probe
NMAP · PORT SCANS02

Know the scan flag

-sTTCP Connect scan
-sSTCP SYN / half-open scan
-sUUDP scan
-sATCP ACK scan
-sNTCP Null scan
-FFast scan; fewer common ports
CALCULATION03

Inclusive IP ranges

For a range from .100 to .115, count both endpoints.

115 − 100 + 1 = 16 hosts

Likewise, --top-ports 20 requests 20 of the most common ports.
